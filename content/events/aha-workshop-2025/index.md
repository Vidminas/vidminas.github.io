abstract: |2-
  Contributed to the Comprehension, Reasoning & Agency track at the [Workshop for Designing Benchmarks for Human Flourishing with AI](https://www.media.mit.edu/events/aha-flourishing-workshop/), hosted by the MIT Media Lab's Advancing Humans with AI (AHA) research program. The workshop brought together around 80 experts from over 40 organisations to draft conversation scenarios, define criteria for good and bad AI responses, and test the resulting benchmark against several large language models.

  The workshop report was later published as [Towards Open Benchmarks for Human Flourishing with AI](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/report-benchmarks-for-human-flourishing-with-ai/overview/).
