  Took part in the Digital Research Ambassadors Programme panel discussion at the University of Edinburgh's second annual [Digital Research Conference](https://digitalresearchservices.ed.ac.uk/digital-research-conference), organised by Digital Research Services. The day mixed short talks, panels, posters, and an expo on how digital tools -- especially AI -- are changing research, and how to use them responsibly.

  After an introduction to the programme, each of us on the panel introduced ourselves and our motivation for joining the scheme, followed by a chaired Q&A with questions from the audience. The discussion centred on experiences of interdisciplinary collaboration and communication, the challenges and growth that come from working beyond your comfort zone, the broader impact of the internship, developing digital skills, how to scale good digital research practice, and advice for the new cohort of ambassadors.

  I was a Digital Research Ambassador in 2023 and 2024, working on an [experimental measurements database for granular materials](/projects/tusail-emdb/) and on [measuring originality in student work written with and without ChatGPT](/projects/drs-genai-originality/).
