abstract: |2-
  Co-authored the research paper "Defining “Open” Artificial Intelligence in Education: Expert Variations", presented by Robert Farrow on Monday 22 June under the theme cluster "Reimagining openness: theory, technology, and futures".

  The paper reports our phenomenographic study of how 24 experts in artificial intelligence and open education understand "openness" in educational AI. Stakeholders' understandings vary widely -- some see AI as fundamentally incompatible with openness, some think it possible but not yet achieved, others point to concrete examples they consider open. We identify a framework of increasing complexity, from a binary "open or closed" view, to a linear scale of "how open", to a multi-dimensional view distinguishing what is open (data, code, model) and to what extent, and finally a contextual understanding incorporating whom those aspects are open to and for what purposes. The aim is to help identify openwashing and support better-informed conversations about open AI in education.

  Written with Vi Truong (Charles Sturt University and University of Melbourne) and Robert Farrow (The Open University).
