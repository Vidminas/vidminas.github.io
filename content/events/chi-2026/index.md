abstract: |2-
  Organised and ran the [Child-Centred AI-Mediated Collaborative Agency by Design (CAMCAD)](https://oxfordhcc.github.io/CAMCAD/) workshop, continuing the series of Child-Centred AI workshops at CHI 2023 and 2024. I assembled the team of ten co-organisers, led the proposal writing, ran the website, call for papers, and submission portal, managed peer review of 15 submissions, and coordinated the co-authored post-workshop report.

  The workshop opened with a keynote from Professor Netta Iivari (University of Oulu) on fostering transformative agency of children in the age of AI, followed by five invited talks, a poster session with fourteen accepted submissions, and group discussion on design responses to the risks AI poses to children's agency.

  The extended abstract is published in the [CHI 2026 Proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3778754), and the recordings, accepted submissions, and [full workshop report](https://oxfordhcc.github.io/CAMCAD/report/) are on the workshop website.
